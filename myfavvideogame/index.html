Marvel's Spider-Man on Playstation

Marvel’s Spider-Manis an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games and released for PlayStation 4 in 2018. Players step into the role of Peter Parker, a longtime superhero balancing life in New York City with the responsibility of protecting it as Spider-Man. The game combines fast-paced combat, web-swinging traversal, exploration, and a story about friendship, responsibility, and difficult choices
