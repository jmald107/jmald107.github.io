Gameplay, Gadgets & New York

Move through the city

Marvel's Spider-Man is played from a third-person perspective. Players swing across Manhattan, climb buildings, complete missions, and stop crimes. Combat rewards movement, timing, and using Spider-Man's gadgets in creative combinations. Between story missions, players can explore the city, take photographs, complete challenges, and unlock upgrades.

Core gameplay loop

  1. Travel through the city using web-swinging and parkour.
  2. Investigate a mission or city activity.
  3. Use dodges, combos, webbing, and gadgets to handle enemies.
  4. Earn rewards and experience to unlock suits and upgrades.
  5. Continue the story or explore more of New York.
